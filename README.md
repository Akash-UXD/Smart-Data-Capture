# 🚀 Smart Data Capture for Sales Reps
> *Saving the 70% time wasted on CRM ops so sales reps can focus on building relationships.*

## 📌 Overview
This project is a product design take-home assignment by **Akash Jaladurgam** for Everstage. It addresses a major bottleneck in B2B SaaS sales: **reps spend 70% of their time on operational tasks** (logging calls, updating CRMs, and writing follow-ups) and only 30% on actual selling.

---

## 🎯 The Core Problem
* **Ops Overload:** Reps waste their day on CRM updates rather than talking to clients.
* **Zero Incentive for Hygiene:** Reps are paid to close deals, not do administrative documentation. 
* **Broken Forecasts:** Incomplete CRM data leads to managers getting blindsided and breaking company revenue forecasts.
* **Misaligned Goals:** A rep's personal goal is to **close deals quickly** for commission, while the business goal is **long-term documentation and data tracking**.

---

## 💡 The Solution
A **Smart Data Capture** system that acts as an intelligent mediator between communication channels (like Zoom, Google Meet, Slack, and Email) and the CRM.

1. **Auto-Capture:** AI listens to calls, reads emails, and extracts context.
2. **AI Documentation:** Automatically drafts CRM updates and summaries based on pipeline stages.
3. **Approval-First Workflow:** Instead of manual typing, reps simply **review and approve** the AI-generated logs in a streamlined inbox.
4. **Enforced Pipeline Progression:** Deals can only move forward to the next pipeline stage once the documentation is approved and synced, turning documentation into the fastest path to commission.
